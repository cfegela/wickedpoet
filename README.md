# How to Play Wicked Poet

**Wicked Poet** is a 1–6 player online multiplayer collaborative poetry game. Write twisted verse together—one hidden stanza at a time—then read the full poems you created.

---

## 1. Setup & Lobby

1. **Create or Join a Room**: One player hosts a room (up to **6 players**, with solo play also supported for testing) and shares the invite link or QR code.
2. **Enter Your Username**: Each player chooses a username—no account or login required.
3. **Start the Game**: Once everyone is seated in the lobby, the host starts Round 1.

---

## 2. Round 1 — Opening Lines

- Every player starts their own poem.
- Write **1 or 2 lines** of poetry, then tap **End Turn** in the HUD.
- Once all players have ended their turn, Round 2 begins.

---

## 3. Round 2+ — Passing the Verse

- The game runs for **double the number of players** in rounds (e.g., 3 players = 6 rounds; 6 players = 12 rounds).
- Each round, poems rotate to the next player: Player 2 sees a **truncated prompt** (only the last few words) of what Player 1 wrote, Player 3 sees Player 2's last few words, and so on.
- Write **1 or 2 lines** to continue the verse and tap **End Turn** in the HUD.

---

## 4. End of Game — Reading the Poem

- Once all rounds are complete, the game moves to the reveal screen.
- Each player receives and reads the **full poem they started** from beginning to end.
